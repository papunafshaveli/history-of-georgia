Privacy Policy for History of Georgia

Last Updated: 05.05.2026

Introduction

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information while using the app.

1. Data Collection

The app collects only the data needed to provide its features:

Anonymous account:
When you first open the app, an anonymous account is created automatically through Firebase Authentication. It is identified by a random user ID and is not linked to any personal information about you.

Optional sign-in (Google or Apple):
You may optionally sign in with Google or Apple to appear on the leaderboard. If you do, the app receives your name and (where available) profile photo from Google or Apple and stores them in Firebase Firestore. Your email address is held by Firebase Authentication and is not stored separately by the app.

Game data:
While you play, the app saves your game results (score, correct answers, total questions, difficulty, and timestamp) and lifetime stats in Firebase Firestore. Settings, recent games, and lifetime statistics are also kept locally on your device.

Push Notifications:
When you grant notification permission, the app generates and stores a device push token in Firebase Firestore. This token is used solely to deliver notifications to your device. It is not linked to any personal information. You can disable notifications at any time through the app's Settings screen or your device notification settings — your token will be deleted from our servers when notifications are turned off.

YouTube Content:
The app provides access to YouTube videos. When you view this content, YouTube (Google LLC) may collect data according to their own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera. We do not use advertising identifiers or third-party analytics.

2. Third-Party Services

- Firebase (Google) — anonymous authentication, optional Google Sign-In, Cloud Firestore database, push token storage: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Sign in with Apple — optional iOS sign-in: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Expo (Expo Application Services) — push token issuance and over-the-air application updates: https://expo.dev/privacy-explained
- YouTube (Google) — displays educational videos: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Push tokens, game results, and account information are stored securely in Firebase, and server-side rules ensure that each user can only access their own data.

4. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app: Settings → Account → "Delete account & sign out". This permanently removes your authentication record, your user document, your game history, and your push tokens from our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. When using third-party services like YouTube, parental guidance is recommended.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted in the app with the updated date.
